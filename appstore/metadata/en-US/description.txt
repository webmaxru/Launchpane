Launchpane helps you understand macOS background services from one focused native interface.

The Mac App Store edition is a sandboxed service inspector and personal LaunchAgent plist editor. Diagnose startup configuration, inspect service state and schedules, and read logs without memorizing plist structure.

FEATURES
• Browse user agents, shared agents, daemons and app login helpers from folders you select
• Search service labels and filter by source
• Inspect running/loaded state, process ID, exit status and configuration
• Review interval and calendar schedules
• Read and refresh configured logs after selecting their containing folder
• Create and edit personal LaunchAgent plists with a guided form
• View and copy Terminal command references
• Follow the system appearance or choose light or dark

FOLDER ACCESS
Choose folders in the native macOS picker. Select your Library/LaunchAgents folder for personal plists, shared LaunchAgents and LaunchDaemons folders for read-only inventory, and Applications for login helpers. Only selected inventory roots are shown. Log access requires selecting the containing folder. Launchpane remembers your chosen folders after you relaunch; use Forget folders to remove them.

EDITION BOUNDARIES
This Store edition does not load, run, stop, restart, enable, disable or remove services, and has no administrator mode. System/shared configuration and login helpers are read-only. Creating or editing a personal plist saves the file only; it does not activate or reload the service.

No account, subscription or network service is required. Service definitions and logs stay on your Mac. Launchpane does not collect your data or include analytics or advertising.
